**Syndication Mod** adds several unique services to the game. These are not
traditional services which buy or sell things in the Market. Instead they
implement Storylines to produce unusual interactions with the Contact. Each
of these services come with 4 Service Levels. The Storyline checks the
Service Level and upgrades the nature of the Service accordingly.

##Gambling
The **Seven Dragons** Casino Owner has a unique service offering a seat in a
high-stakes gambling game. At some point, a Contact involved in Entertainment
informs the Cyber Knight that a Casino Owner wants to talk to the Knight. The
introduces themself and invites the Knight to join the game, open only to
"movers and shakers" like the Knight.

A chat button will appear on the Casino Owners profile page. Opening the chat
presents the player with a choice of how much money to put down as a stake.
At Service Level 1, only $100k is available. Higher Service Levels add options
for $200k, $400k, and $800k. The money is withdrawn from the account
immediately and the Knight become unavailable for 1 day. At the end of the
game, the player learns how well they did. They could lose the entire stake,
or lose 50%, lose 25%, break even, gain 50%, gain 100%, or gain 300%. The
results are weighted to average at breaking even in the long term.

The game is popular so the Casino Owner can only fit the Knight into a game
once in a while. After placing a stake, the Cyber Knight cannot join another
high-stakes game for 30 days.

###Future Development
In the future, the Knight will get additional benefits from playing the game.
At the end of each game there will be a chance to meet new Contacts, get a
clue to a potential job, and perhaps other events.

##One-Time Sales
The **Ricksham** Railmaster will offer the chance to buy rare products, a 
one-time offer. This Contact and Service are not yet implemented.

###Future Development
The service will be initiated by a normal proc-gen storyline, handled by the
Casting Director. When triggered, the Railmaster will announce (on the
Timeline) a unique opportunity to buy a rare product. The product will be
added to the Marketplace as a One-Time Service selling a single product -
you can only buy one of them. It will also be a limited-time offer. After 1
week, the Service will be removed. The product will be chosen based on the
Team Power Level and the Service Level. The product will probably be selected
from loot-only gear and blueprint-only gear. It may include Black Site gear.

I may add an additional restriction that each product can go on sale one time.

##Moneylending
The Money Lender Mongrel from **Que Milieu** will become available in all
games and gain a  Moneylending Service. This is not yet implemented in the
mod.

###Future Development
The Moneylending service will be exactly what it sounds like. A new chat will
appear on Mongrel's profile page. Opening the chat will present the player
with a choice of how much money to borrow. The choices will be limited by
Service Level, with higher Service Levels giving options for larger loans.

The loan should be paid back within some period of time, perhaps 3 months. I 
am considering options for handling default. Some options might be:

- Add Heat and other debuffs to encourage payment
- Extend the loan, doubling the amount to be repaid
- Send Head Hunters
- Have Que Milieu demand the Knight fill a dangerous job to pay off the loan

##Strong-Arm Enforcement
The **Yakuza** Syndicate Blade will offer some kind of Service involving
strong-arm enforcement. I am still working on this concept.
