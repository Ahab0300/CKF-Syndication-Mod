#  Syndication Mod Documentation

The **Syndication Mod** expands the Syndicate Factions in CKF. In the base game, only the D-Cartel syndicate
and Que Milieu syndicate can offer jobs or be the target of jobs. The Ricksham syndicate has a
prominent role in drug imports but little impact on the game environment. A couple other small
dead or dying syndicates are also mentioned and have even less impact.

- [Factions](Factions.md)
- [Services](Services.md)
- [Roadmap](Roadmap.md)

Syndication Mod adds the following to the base game:

* Ricksham syndicate is available to the Casting Director for missions as actor or target
* Seven Dragons syndicate (present in the base game data but undeveloped) is available to the Casting Director
* The Yakuza (a syndicate faction in Cyber Knights Classic) is added to the game and available to the Casting Director
* New Contacts added for Ricksham, Seven Dragons, and Yakuza (not yet available)
* Gambling Service from Seven Dragons Casino Owner
* Replica Weapon Mods Service from Ricksham Railmaster

Future development may add some or all of the following:
* Two or three unique enemy types for each of the five big Syndicates
* Unique services for Yakuza and Que Milieu Syndicates
